Privacy policy

11.10.2026

1. Controller and contact

The controller of Exp-lore data is Paweł Gąszczyk. Contact: contact-exploreapp@gmail.com.

2. Data we store

The game stores a character nickname and code, settings, the virtual hero’s position, items, currency, quests, explored map, statistics and last-play date. An optional account uses email and password or Google sign-in. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider. Rankings, the newspaper and memorial may display nicknames, achievements and places visited by the character.

3. Device location

GPS is optional. Your browser asks for permission after you press the location button during character creation. It helps choose a nearby starting address. Exact phone coordinates are not stored as a device position; the selected character home is saved and may reveal your area. You can enter another place instead. The hero’s position does not continuously track your phone.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Account and progress data support the game service (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Diagnostics, security and correspondence support the controller’s legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). Bug reports may include a description, game state, version, browser information and an optional screenshot. Editorial letters include text and character information. Crash reports help fix the game.

5. Retention

Accounts and characters are kept until a deletion request. Diagnostics retain the latest 5,000 crashes, 3,000 bug reports and screenshots from the latest 500 reports. We do not claim a fixed retention period for letters and public summaries; contact the controller for details.

6. Providers and browser storage

Supabase provides database and authentication in Frankfurt, EU. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare serve the site and assets and may process IP addresses and request data. Google provides fonts and optional sign-in; AWS serves public terrain elevation data. MET Norway weather is requested for an approximate area without player account data. Depending on the provider, technical data may be processed outside the EEA under its service terms and applicable safeguards. We use no advertising analytics. The game uses browser storage for settings and sessions; this newspaper stores only your language preference as exp-language.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, or object where GDPR provides these rights. You may complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO). Contact the address above. Avoid real names in nicknames and personal details in public letters. Younger players should use accounts and email contact with a guardian’s help.
