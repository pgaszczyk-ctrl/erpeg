Privacy policy

8 November 2026

Exp-lore is a project within Magicownia.pl.

1. Controller and contact

The data controller is Paweł Gąszczyk, who runs Exp-lore as part of the Magicownia.pl project. Contact for all personal data matters: contact-exploreapp@gmail.com.

2. In short

  • You don't need to give an e-mail address or your real name to play – a character only has an invented name and a code.
  • There are no ads, tracking cookies or analytics tools. We don't sell data or share it for marketing.
  • The game's database is in the European Union (Supabase, Frankfurt).

3. What we store and why

  • Character: name (nickname), character code, difficulty, appearance and language – so you can play and load your character.
  • Save data: your hero's position on the game map, items, currency, quest progress, discovered places, statistics (such as distance walked) and the date you last played – so your character is where you left it and the game rules work.
  • Account (optional): e-mail address and password (stored only in encrypted form by the sign-in provider) or Google sign-in data (e-mail address, name, profile picture) – for signing in and listing your characters.
  • Visible to other players: your character's name, level, achievements and daily results (distance walked, in-game country and town) in the rankings of the newspaper “The Steam Courier”; in the Arena other players may fight a “ghost” of your character (name, level, equipment).
  • Content you send yourself: bug reports (description, game state, game version, device and browser model, recent in-game events and – unless you untick it – a screenshot of the game), letters to the editors, ideas from city notice boards, requests for new routes, and the fact that you solved the puzzle of the day.
  • Diagnostics: automatic reports about program errors and slowdowns (game version, browser, graphics settings, approximate device memory and number of cores, battery level) – to fix bugs and make the game smoother.
  • Device location (GPS): only if you press the location button while creating a character. The game turns it into a nearby place on the game map and stores that as the character's starting place; we do not store your phone's exact coordinates. The stored starting place may still indicate your area – if you don't want that, type another place. The game does not track your phone's position.

Legal bases: performance of the contract, i.e. providing the game and account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); security, bug fixing and handling correspondence – the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

4. Data on your device

The game stores settings (such as language, picture sharpness, zoom, sound), photos taken with the in-game camera and – if you sign in – account session data in your browser's storage. The cookie exp-postac (up to 2 years) remembers the name and code of your last character so that the “Continue” button works; remove it with “Forget this character on this device” or by clearing the site's data. These are not tracking or advertising cookies. This newspaper stores only your language choice (exp-language).

5. Service providers

  • Supabase – database and sign-in (servers in Frankfurt, EU).
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare – website hosting, domain and map files; like any server they see your IP address and technical request data.
  • Google – fonts (Google Fonts) loaded by your browser and, if you choose it, Google sign-in.
  • Amazon Web Services – public terrain height files loaded by your browser.
  • MET Norway – weather forecasts; the request is sent by our server, only with an approximate area (a square of about 30 km around your hero in the game), with no data about you.
  • itch.io – if you play Exp-lore embedded on itch.io, that site processes data on its own pages under its own privacy policy.

Some providers (GitHub, Cloudflare, Google, Amazon) are based in the USA; data is transferred under GDPR mechanisms such as standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep data

  • Characters, save data and accounts – as long as you use them or until you ask for deletion.
  • Program error reports – the latest 5,000 entries; bug reports – the latest 3,000, with screenshots kept only for the newest 500 (older ones are deleted automatically).
  • Daily rankings and puzzle solutions – 12 months.
  • Letters to the editors, ideas and route requests – 24 months.

7. Children

The game is also meant for children and playing requires no personal data. Use a nickname, not a real first and last name. An e-mail or Google account for a child under 16 should be created by a parent or guardian. A parent may ask at any time for a child's data to be deleted.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable form and object to processing. Write to contact-exploreapp@gmail.com and give your character's name (and your account e-mail, if you have one). You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Poland the President of UODO (uodo.gov.pl) – or the authority in your country.

9. Changes

We will announce changes to this policy on this page and in the in-game newspaper. The rules for using the game are in the Terms of Service.
