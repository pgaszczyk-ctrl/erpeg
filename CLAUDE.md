# Erpeg: notes for Claude

- The owner does not program. They describe features in Polish; reply in Polish, avoid jargon, and verify changes yourself (build plus a Playwright screenshot/check) before reporting.
- Stack: Phaser 4 + TypeScript + Vite. `npm run build` runs a typecheck and the build. The Phaser 4 API differs from v3 (e.g. `setTintMode` instead of `setTintFill`); the bundled guides live in `node_modules/phaser/skills/`.
- The game is played on the real map of Lublin. `data/lublin-osm.geojsonseq.gz` is an OpenStreetMap extract made by `scripts/fetch-osm.sh`. Refresh it with the "Fetch Lublin map data" workflow (workflow_dispatch); the sandbox cannot reach OSM servers. `scripts/build-map.mjs` turns it into `public/map/lublin.json` (gitignored) before dev/build.
- Layout: `src/content/fabula.ts` holds the missions and enemy spots, written in Polish by address; the owner dictates these. `src/map/CityMap.ts` handles collisions, address lookup and spawn. `src/map/MapRenderer.ts` draws the map in chunks. `src/quests.ts` holds the logged-in session and mission progress. `src/api.ts` talks to the server. `src/ui/menu.ts` is the HTML start screen (new character, load, memorial). `src/art.ts` holds the placeholder sprites. `src/objects/` holds the player and enemies (plain sprites with `vel`, moved by GameScene with map collisions). `src/scenes/` holds Boot (loads the map), Game and UI (HUD, dialogs, goal arrow, touch controls).
- When adding missions, check that the addresses exist in the map data (the game also shows a toast listing any it cannot find).
- Mobile first: integer camera zoom. All input (touch, keyboard, mouse click) is handled natively in `src/controls.ts`; Phaser input for touch and keyboard is disabled because its pointer and key state got stuck on real devices. UIScene only draws the controls. Test at phone viewport too.
- Scale: `PX_PER_M` (1.92) in CityMap. Stored positions carry their scale (players.map_scale, snapshot `s`, fog `s`) and are converted on load.
- Fog of war: `src/map/Fog.ts`. Vision is a raycast cone blocked by buildings. Explored cells are saved in the save as `fog`. Enemies and pickups outside the vision are hidden. The map screen is `src/ui/minimap.ts` (🗺 button or M).
- Shops (every OSM shop=supermarket/discount whatever the chain, so it works abroad too; in towns also named convenience shops) and schools come from OSM POIs (`pois` in the map JSON, `CityMap.places`). Shops sell swords and schools teach sword fighting; prices and stats live in `src/content/sklepy.ts`. Sword and skill are stored in the save. Entering a shop or school is a save point too. Separate sidewalk/crossing ways are dropped in build-map; car roads are drawn with a stone sidewalk edge.
- Churches, offices and police stations give random missions (`src/content/zlecenia.ts`, `missionForPlace` in quests.ts). The seed is place id + a per-login nonce, so each login brings a new one. Taken, unfinished ones are kept in the save as `gen`. Police gives bounties: a bandit hidden near a spot (`szukaj`: the arrow shows only the area) or a big slime. Hospitals heal for free. Enemy kinds are in `ENEMY_KINDS` (objects/Slime.ts).
- Map look: build-map drops roofs/shelters and tiny buildings (<25 m² without address), grows every building by 0.7 m, merges touching ones and cuts every road out as wide as it is drawn (clipper-lib, ~5 min; skipped when the JSON is newer than the data and the script). All roads, pavements and paths are drawn as one track: every outline first, then every fill in one colour.
- `src/scenes/Ambient.ts`: fruit trees on green areas (deterministic per area, created near the hero; swinging drops one fruit, 2–5 per tree, regrow each login; fruit sold in shops, prices in content/sklepy.ts) and street enemies (0–20 per 1×1 km cell by narrow-street length, in groups; a chasing enemy pulls in its neighbours; enemies far from the hero sleep).
- Headless Chromium frame times vary a lot between containers; compare against an older build on the same machine before calling something a regression.
- Items and skills: `src/content/przedmioty.ts` (catalogue: weapon in hand, ranged bow or magic item, armour, helmet, boots; skill curve 100 × 1.5^n up to level 10). `src/inventory.ts` holds `gear`: equip, a 5-slot bag (fruit stacks to 99), skill practice points and the magic flag. Saved as equip/bag/skills/magic; old sword/swordSkill/fruits saves are converted in loadGear. A tap is a melee swing; holding over 250 ms (or dragging) aims and releasing shoots (`hold`/`consumeRelease` in controls.ts, `updateRanged`/`updateShots` in GameScene). Hits on enemies, trees and training stations (dummy/target/crystal on sports pitches, `Training` in Ambient.ts) give practice. Libraries teach magic and sell magic items; schools sell lessons (+practice). Character sheet: `src/ui/character.ts` (👤 or C).
- Admin panel: `admin.html` + `src/admin/admin.ts` (no Phaser). Auth: admin password checked by `_admin_ok` (bcrypt in `admin_config`, 5 bad tries lock 10 min); RPCs `admin_overview`, `admin_save_mission`, `admin_save_code`, `admin_set_key`. Missions made there live in table `missions` (same fields as `Misja`), are loaded at login by `game_content` (`loadContent` in quests.ts, 5 s timeout) and merged with MISJE. Zadanie typ `brak` = just a place.
- Secret codes: table `secret_codes` (code, mission_id, reward item, max_uses) + `redemptions` (once per character). In a mission dialog whose id has an active code the game adds "🤫 Psst, mam tajemne hasło" (`missionDialog`/`tellSecret` in GameScene, prompt in `src/ui/prompt.ts`), RPC `redeem_code` (10 bad tries per hour). Mythic items in przedmioty.ts have `efekt`: `swiatlo` (vision ×SWIATLO, glow) and `pioruny` (auto lightning, PIORUNY).
- Death and resurrection: `die` stores death_x/y/scale and stats and adds a row to `deaths` (the memorial board reads `deaths`). The start screen shows a dead character's ghost on a still map (`ghostMap` in menu.ts, `drawCity` in `src/map/drawCity.ts`). `resurrect` is free the first time; later ones answer "1 €, payments soon".
- Stats in the save (`stats`: m walked, kills per kind, earned, spent, fruit, missions, codes), counted via `earn`/`spend` in quests.ts. Eating 20 fruit heals one heart (character sheet). Practice hits show a skill bar (UIScene `showPractice`).
- Home: a house at the start point (TEX.home). Coming back to it heals fully, saves and offers the chest (`src/ui/chest.ts`): 10×10 slots + money, saved as `chest` {slots, coins}; drag with pointer events or tap to move between chest and backpack.
- Riddle-givers ("mądrale", `src/scenes/Npcs.ts`, content in `src/content/zagadki.ts`): every day about one in 4 schools/churches gets one on a street ~500 m away (seeded by date). One riddle a day each, one try, picked for the player's age (players.age, asked at creation, default 7) and the NPC's difficulty (−1/0/+1 level); half are generated sums. Answers kept in the save as `riddles` {npcId: day}.
- Languages: `src/i18n.ts` picks Polish or English from the device (`?lang=pl|en` forces it); two-language texts are `{ pl, en }` read with `tr()`, or inline `tx(pl, en)`. So far only the fixed characters are bilingual; the rest of the game is still Polish.
- Fixed characters (`src/content/postacie.ts`, `src/scenes/FixedNpcs.ts`, they stroll along their street lines): the dog on Guliwera/Cyda (piggy in bushes near the playground between them, state `missions['npc-pies']`, +100 EXP once), Sister Margo on Orlanda (riddle on the 3rd talk of the day, then 2 manners riddles, 10 EXP each), Grandpa Marek or Grandma Iwonka (by day) at Jana Śnieżyńskiego 19–27 (one science riddle a day, 50 EXP). Daily counters in the save as `daily`.
- HUD (UIScene): top right the hero's portrait (hero-me head, tap = character sheet), hearts under it (count by difficulty: `serca` in trudnosc.ts, 5/5/5/3/2, set as PLAYER.maxHp at login), then 5 experience stars filling by halves towards the next level (TEX.star/starHalf/starEmpty); coins and 🗺 left of the portrait; weapon/fruit top left. Levelling up shows a big "Poziom N!" splash (`levelUp`). The melee swing goes exactly the way the hero last walked (any angle). Libraries: "🧩 Zagadka bibliotekarki", BIBLIOTEKA_ZAGADKI (3 per session, 10 EXP, `session.libRiddles`). Training dummies: pitches from `kuklyOdM2` (400 m²), only the biggest of neighbours within `sasiedziM` (`hasStations`); the coach and far dummy only on big pitches. `window.__session` is exposed for tests.
- Item pictures: 16×16 pixel-art PNGs in `public/items/<item id>.png` (made by the owner with another AI: 1 px #1e1a24 outline, transparent, no anti-aliasing), shown enlarged with `image-rendering: pixelated` by `src/ui/itemIcon.ts` (list `HAVE`) in the character sheet and the chest (empty slots show a greyed one, `EMPTY_PIC`), and as Phaser textures `item-<id>` (loaded in BootScene.preload, `itemTexture`) on shop/library buttons (`DialogRequest.icons`); items without a picture fall back to emoji. New items need a PNG and an entry in `ITEM_PICTURES`.
- Hero look: `src/look.ts` draws the player's hero pixel by pixel (16×20 frames: head shape, build, outfit, hairstyle, skin/hair/top/bottom colours, plus worn helmet/headwear, armour and boots). Chosen with sliders at creation (preview in menu.ts), saved as `save.look` (create_character `p_look`). In the game the texture is `hero-me` with anims `me-walk-*` (`makePlayerTexture` in art.ts), redrawn when worn gear changes (`refreshLook`). NPCs still use the default `hero` texture. Headwear items: kapelusz (Robin Hood), skórzany/żelazny hełm, czapka_maga (library), korona (only via codes/rewards); shops offer every headwear not owned.
- Home: the nearest real building to the start point (`setUpHome`), a small house icon on its roof; a sword swing that lands on it opens the home (walking in does not). No enemies within `HOME_SAFE_M` (40 m) of the start: they vanish (`clearHomeArea`, mission enemies excepted).
- Roads: build-map turns major/medium/minor roads into `paved` areas (merged, gaps under 12 m between them closed, buildings cut out), drawn with a cobblestone pattern over the earthen tracks; thinner roads and paths stay earthen. Roundabouts (`junction=roundabout`) get a `merchant` POI: a cart on the ring that works as a shop.
- Fog: a building hit by a vision ray is shown whole (roof and walls) and marked explored (`markBuilding`).
- Touch: the joystick (with arrows) appears wherever the first touch that is not on the attack button lands and disappears when the thumb lifts; speed grows with the distance from its centre. A quick tap on the joystick (under 260 ms, under 10 px of drag) is also a sword swing, for one-handed play; the attack button (`attackHome`, half size) is for two hands. A ghost joystick blinks at the start and after ~9 s without moving (`joyHome`/`activity` in controls.ts). The arrows are a plain Graphics (inside a Container it did not follow the position).
- Sharing the code: "📤 Wyślij sobie" uses the phone's share menu (Web Share API, with the QR picture where allowed), falling back to a wa.me link.
- Difficulty: a slider at creation (`src/content/trudnosc.ts`: Dziecięcy (default), Młody, Średni, Wysoki, Hardkor), stored as an age in players.age (6/9/12/18/19, `trudnoscZWieku` maps back; old characters keep their typed age). It sets the riddle level, enemy damage (fractions add up in `damageCarry`), vision around/behind (`back` in visionPolygon), a wide sword sweep in degrees (`inArc`/`drawSweep`), street-enemy density and enemy speed (`tempo` → `Slime.tempo`). Enemies stop at the hero's edge; a swing also hits an enemy standing on the hero; shots start at the hero and check their whole path each frame. `session.level` holds it.
- Talking to characters (fixed ones, riddle-givers, townsfolk, sports people, the wizard) starts with a sword swing that lands on them or next to the hero (`talkableAt` in resolveAttack; an enemy in reach wins), not by bumping into them. For `TALK_PAUSE_MS` (700 ms) after any dialog closes a swing doesn't start a talk (the tap that closed it would reopen it).
- Character level (content/historia.ts): max MAKS_POZIOM_POSTACI 20 (EXP thresholds 50·(N−1)·N). PREMIA_POZIOMU: at 20 twice the life and +30% walking speed, part = ((level−1)/19)^0.77 (≈30% of it by level 5, 80% by 15). `zyciePostaci`/`szybkoscPostaci`; PLAYER.maxHp set at login and by GameScene.applyLevel (new hearts come filled), Player.speed (used by the coach/runner timings too). HUD hearts wrap in rows of 5; level 20 shows "(max)"; the sheet shows the bonus.
- Library surveys (BIBLIOTEKA_MAPA in content/zlecenia.ts, `mapMissionForLibrary` in quests.ts): a learned society asks to find the way to a village 2–30 km away (`CityMap.settlements()`: addresses without a street, ≥ 8 houses); reward per km (25 coins, 60 EXP). Reaching it shows `zadanie.komunikat` in a dialog; `dowolnaBiblioteka` missions are handed in at any library ("📜 Oddaj relację", the arrow points to the nearest one).
- Quests: at most `ZADAN_NARAZ` (3) at once, counting the main story (Cień smoka, `story.goal()`, gold ⭐), sports challenges and missions in progress (`activeQuests`/`sideQuests` in GameScene; `questsFull` blocks taking another, a new mission's dialog then only says so). Each side quest keeps an arrow colour from KOLORY_ZADAN (content/fabula.ts) while active (`questColors`); HudState.quests → up to 3 coloured goal lines and arrows (UIScene, `arrowTexture` in art.ts), all on the minimap too; the character sheet shows the active ones as a quest log (done ones are not shown).
- Map streaming (Lublin): build-maps writes the whole map to `.cache/lublin-full.json` (not published), then `scripts/split-map.mjs` (runs the game's own CityMap in Node with --experimental-strip-types) cuts it into an index `public/map/lublin.json` (boundary, paved/big areas, `bld` = every building with an address or name as a stub with bbox + precomputed door, `places`, `streets` = start point per street name) and 1 km tiles `public/map/lublin/<cx>_<cy>.json` (areas/lines/buildings with their full-map `id`; a feature is in every tile it touches, deduped by id on load). CityMap tile mode: `ensure(x, y, r)` fetches tiles, `ready(box)`, `onTile` (MapRenderer redraws those chunks in place), unloaded tiles are blocked in `isBlocked`, stubs get their rings when their tile arrives (same object, so place.building identity holds), `addressed()` for random mission targets, seeds/draw order use `id`. Loading: `prepareMap` (travel.ts) before a scene starts (1 km around arrival/home/unfinished-session spot, 400 m around fixed characters' streets), GameScene.update keeps `LOAD_RADIUS` (2 km) loaded; per-cell caches (Townsfolk, StreetEnemies, Forest) skip cells that aren't `ready`; minimap, ghost map, admin preview and the dragon spot fetch what they need first. Towns stay single files.
- The Lublin map is the city boundary plus 8 km circles around Motycz-Józefin, Jastków and Garbów (`scripts/lublin-area.json`, union in build-map `withCircles`; fetch-osm.sh BBOX covers them), so they are reachable on foot along every real road. Its projection origin is a fixed `anchor` (the original city map's NW corner), so old saved positions stay valid; the new parts have negative coordinates: the JSON has `x0`/`y0` (≤ 0) and `origin`, CityMap `minX`/`minY` (camera bounds, isBlocked). Stations inside those circles count as Lublin stations (plan-towns). New characters can pick the start with 📍 (geolocation, `city.hasLatLon`); outside the Lublin map: "Niestety nie znamy jeszcze tej lokalizacji" and Plac Zamkowy.
- Region (Lubelszczyzna): the whole region as one map is far too big, so every railway station/halt outside Lublin gets a small town map (~2.6 km square, nearby stations share one). `scripts/fetch-towns.sh` (run by the fetch workflow) plans them (`plan-towns.mjs` → data/towns.json), cuts each out one at a time (all at once ran the runner out of memory) into data/towns/<id>.geojsonseq.gz, and reads OSM train routes (route=train relations, stops in order) into data/rail.json (`rail-from-osm.mjs`: which station follows which; the mkuran.pl GTFS feeds returned 404). Stations over the border (Cyrillic names) are skipped. `scripts/build-maps.mjs` builds Lublin + every town (public/map/towns/<id>.json, build-map `--bbox`) and public/map/world.json. In the game `src/travel.ts` loads world.json and caches maps; stations are `station` places with a coachman (TEX.coach). `openCoach`/`travel` in GameScene offer the next and after-next stations (price COACH_FEE + COACH_PER_KM/km; the coachman always waits, no departure hours, as the owner asked; without route data, the 3 nearest stations; at most 2 destinations on the same map), then restart the scene on the new map (`session.mapId`, `session.arrive`). Town place/NPC ids are prefixed `<town>/`; fog per town in the save as `fogs`. Only Lublin has home, story/admin missions and fixed NPCs; snapshot replay only when it was left on the map the login starts on; dying in a town puts the ghost at home.
- Hotels (OSM tourism=hotel/hostel/guest_house/motel, `hotel` places): the load point. `src/hotels.ts` `pickHotels` keeps one random (seeded, always the same) hotel per 300 m square; `src/content/hotele.ts` lists fixed ones (partners) that always stay and win their square, plus HOTEL_CENA (500). Sleeping there costs HOTEL_CENA, heals, saves and sets `save.at` {m: map, x, y, s}; hitting home clears it. Login (`enterWorld` in travel.ts) starts at `save.at` (loading that town map) or at home. No teleports. Other save points still save progress but don't move the load point.
- Earning: forests (`Forest` in Ambient.ts, LAS in content/sklepy.ts) hold mushrooms (pickups, walk over them; up to `grzybowNaKratke` 3 per cell, plenty on the owner's request) and pines (4 blows = a log) on a 30 m grid, seeded per map; both come back each login. Mushrooms (`grzyb`, edible) and wood (`drewno`, not edible: OWOCE[].jadalne) stack in the bag like fruit and sell in shops ("Sprzedaj zbiory"). Churches/offices also give `zbierz` missions (bring N mushrooms/wood from the nearest forest, ZBIERANIE in zlecenia.ts; goal when the bag holds enough, `takeFruit` on turn-in). Banks (OSM amenity=bank, content/banki.ts): deposits for 1/3/7 days at 5/20/60 %, max 3, saved as `lokaty`; time comes from the server (RPC `server_now`) so the phone clock can't cheat. Coach tickets: COACH_FEE 100 + 30/km (10× on the owner's request).
- Main story "Cień smoka" (`src/scenes/Story.ts`, texts in `src/content/historia.ts`, state in the save as `story` {st, walked, target, dragon, choice, title}): after 60 s of walking the hero stops, a "!" appears, he looks around, a dragon shadow (TEX.dragonShadow, seen from above, head first in the direction of flight) flies over (screech from `src/sfx.ts`, WebAudio) and nearby enemies flee (`fleeUntil`). Schools, churches and universities get "🐉 Zapytaj o cienie" (added to their dialog via `storyPlace` in GameScene.dialog); half of them know (seeded by place id) and send the hero to the nearest university (OSM amenity=university/college, `university` places) or the town hall. There Mag Albrecht (a look-drawn sprite, `makeLookTexture`, czapka_maga) talks from character level 5 (`poziomPostaci`: 100/300/600/1000 EXP, shown in the HUD) and places the dragon (enemy kind `smok`, `peaceful` until the choice) at a free spot far from buildings and from the town centre (mean of all places), 0.5–6 km away. Meeting it: "!" and a choice: fight (title "Pogromca smoka") or study it and talk (4 pages, title "Brat smoków"); the title shows in the HUD and character sheet.
- Enemies: every enemy picture gets a red outline (`redOutline` in art.ts) so bandits aren't mistaken for people. The basic enemy is now a chochlik (imp; internal id still `glut`, texture TEX.slime) and `wielki_glut` a big imp; texts say "chochliki". Street enemies depend on the place (`kindAt` in StreetEnemies): `driada` in forests, `szkielet` within 30 m of a cemetery, `zombie` within 30 m of water (`CityMap.areaNear`/`nearWater`), else imps (10 % bandits). Similar strength and EXP; loot (`dropLoot`): dryads only hearts/fruit, skeletons 2–3 coins and no hearts, the rest as imps.
- Buildings (shops, schools, churches, mission doors…) open with a sword swing at the door or while standing at it (`openDoorAt` in resolveAttack), not by walking in. Enemies can't hurt the hero during a dialog (the scene is paused) nor for 1.5 s after one (`noHurtUntil`). Blinking talk bubbles (TEX.talkBubble, `updateBubbles`) over characters with something today: the dog until its quest is done, Margo/grandparents while riddles are left (`FixedNpcs.important`), the wizard while the story sends the hero to him, sports people; riddle-givers keep their own "?" bubble, now blinking.
- Map drawing: `ringsPath` in MapRenderer skips rings (holes) outside the chunk being painted (`clip`); the merged paved areas have thousands of building holes and each chunk took ~230 ms, now ~17 ms.
- Townsfolk (`src/scenes/Townsfolk.ts`, texts in `src/content/mieszkancy.ts`): 2 people per 200 m of paved street (major/medium/minor lines) in the centre, fewer on estates and suburbs by how many places the 1 km square has (`dzielnice`), and at night (`noc`, also 1.5× street monsters) even fewer; made per 1 km square near the hero (seeded); nobody within `strachPrzedSmokiem` of the story's dragon, strolling along their street (`Walker` from FixedNpcs), 10 shared looks (`folk<n>` + red-outlined `folk<n>-red`). 90 % only say hello (40 % of them tell an errand by the weekday – bank/shop, Saturday shop, Sunday church, Friday a pitch – and walk there along `roadPath`, `errandFor`/`sendTo`), 5 % challenge the hero, 5 % accept a challenge. A duel (`startDuel`/`endDuel` in GameScene) swaps the person for a `wojownik` enemy: life = 6 × `pojedynek` (trudnosc.ts: 0.3/0.5/0.7/1/1.5) × the hero's melee damage, blows = `pojedynek` purple half-hearts; the hero fights on 3 purple hearts (HUD, in place of the weapon line). Losing costs one real heart (never death); winning gives 15 EXP and `stats.duels` (character sheet). Walking 320 px away ends it.
- Sports (`Training` in Ambient.ts, texts/params in `src/content/sport.ts`, difficulty `wyzwanie`/`rywal`/`farta` in trudnosc.ts): training stations only on big pitches (≥ 2500 m²), with a second dummy up to 60 m away and Trener Zbyszek: hit dummy A 10×, hit dummy B, come back, against a timer from the distances × slack (`talkSport`/`dummyHit`/`updateChallenge`/`endChallenge` in GameScene, HUD goal shows the countdown and the arrow the next step). Small pitches have Biegaczka Ania instead (at most one per 1 km square: the small pitch nearest the square's middle, `runnerPitchOf`): a race to another pitch 150–700 m away; her time = the hero's rough street time × `rywal` (with chance `farta` a lucky 0.8×); she runs the shortest street route (`CityMap.roadPath`, A* over road lines around both points, ~5 ms) and her time is that route at the hero's speed × `rywal`.
- Power stone (`KAMIEN_MOCY` in content/sklepy.ts, save `kamienie`): churches sell it for 1 000 000 000 coins or 10 zł ("payments soon"). Dying with one crumbles it and revives the hero at the load point (`reviveWithStone`), no death reported. Character sheet shows level, km walked and stones.
- Selling goods: on Dziecięcy every shop buys fruit/mushrooms/wood; `skup` in trudnosc.ts (0.6 Młody, 0.4 from Średni up) sets the share of shops (seeded by shop id) that do.
- Error log: `src/errlog.ts` sends uncaught errors, failed promises and freezes (a Worker watchdog: page silent or no new frames for 6 s while visible) to RPC `log_error` → table `client_errors` (last 5000). - Rules: Saves happen only when entering a mission building or finishing a mission. Death is final and goes to the memorial board. Closing the tab without "Wyjdź" means the next login replays the last heartbeat snapshot: the character is stuck for 10 s among the enemies that were near. "Wyjdź" is refused while in combat, and the next session starts at the last hotel slept in (or home).
